Privacy.

What this site keeps about you, and what it does not. In short: no cookies, no tracking, and a telegram is kept only so that Stefano can answer it.

Who keeps it

This is the site of Stefano Doko, a graphic designer and web developer in Albania. He reads what you send and answers it. Write to him about anything on this page: hello@example.invalid

A telegram

When you send a telegram from the contact page, the site keeps what you wrote: your name, your business if you give it, the message, where to reply (an email or a phone number), the language to reply in, and when you sent it. It is kept for Stefano to read and answer, and for nothing else; nobody else sees it, and it stays until he deletes it.

If you came through a link that says where it was shared (a poster, a post, a flyer), that name is added to your telegram, so that Stefano knows how you found him.

Against spam

So that nobody can send hundreds of telegrams, the site counts how many arrive from the same internet address in an hour. The count is deleted when the hour is up.

In your browser

A few of your choices are kept in your own browser, never on the server: the language you chose, the night edition if you chose it, whether you stopped the press, and your best score in the game. Until you close the tab it also remembers that you have seen the opening counter and, if a shared link brought you, where it was shared. Clearing the site’s data in your browser clears all of it.

Cookies

None. The site sets no cookies for its visitors and follows nobody. The only cookie is the admin panel’s, and only Stefano signs in there.

Counting visits

Visits are not counted.

Where it is kept

The site and the telegrams are kept on Cloudflare, which serves the site. Its fonts and pictures are on the site itself, so nothing is fetched from other companies while you read, apart from the visit counter when it is on. Tirana’s weather in the engraving comes from MET Norway, the Norwegian weather service: the site’s own server asks for it every half hour, so your browser never contacts them.

Your say

Write to Stefano to see what the site has kept from you, to correct it or to have it deleted.
