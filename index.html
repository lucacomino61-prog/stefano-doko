About Stefano

Stefano Doko is a graphic designer and web developer based in Albania. He makes businesses look good, and he proves it with businesses you can visit. Elixir is an online perfume shop: designer, Arabic and niche fragrances, delivered across Albania. Bar Martiri, in Spille, has ice cream and sunbeds by the sea, and a site in three languages. Both sites are live and in use today. Look at them closely; the loupe on this sheet is there for that. He designs a website and writes its code, front end and back end, in the languages set out on the next sheet. If your business needs to look its best, write to Stefano, in Albanian or in English.
